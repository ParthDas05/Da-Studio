01 / APPOINTMENT AND SCOPE

Nature of Atelier Engagement

These Terms and Conditions govern all architectural design consultations, BIM modeling, interior architecture, and turnkey construction management services rendered by DA STUDIOS ("Atelier", "Contractor", "We") to the client ("Patron", "Property Owner", "You").

Every commissioned project is governed by an executed Individual Commission Agreement (ICA) incorporating the detailed Bill of Quantities (BOQ), architectural drawings, and schedule milestones. In the event of any specific clause variance, the executed ICA takes precedence.

02 / FEASIBILITY ESTIMATES AND LOCKED BOQ

Cost Modeling and Financial Rigor

A. Interactive Specifier Estimates: Any cost ranges generated via our digital Feasibility Specifier or initial consultation notes are indicative budgetary models based on prevailing 2026 Vadodara construction rates. They do not constitute a formal commercial tender.

B. Locked Bill of Quantities: Prior to site mobilization or structural cutting, DA STUDIOS prepares a comprehensive, line-item Bill of Quantities specifying square footages, exact material specs, hardware origins, and finishing methods. Once signed by the client, the base rates for specified items remain locked for the duration agreed in the contract schedule.

C. Client-Initiated Alterations: Any structural redesign, layout revision, or material upgrade requested by the client subsequent to BOQ execution will be documented in a written Change Order Notice detailing cost and timeline adjustments prior to execution.

03 / MATERIAL HONESTY & NATURAL VARIATIONS

Inherent Characteristics of Minerals and Timber

DA STUDIOS works predominantly with authentic architectural materials including natural Navona Travertine, Italian marble, smoked oak, Burmese teak, cast bronze, and artisanal slaked lime plasters. The client acknowledges that:

  • Natural stone exhibits fissures, colour striations, vein clustering, and geological pitting inherent to natural quarry blocks. These characteristics constitute authentic mineral provenance and are not defects.
  • Master dry-lay inspections are conducted prior to cutting at Kishangarh or Carrara warehouses. The client is invited to review slab sequencing prior to final resin filling and surface calibration.
  • Solid wood veneers respond to humidity and seasonal climatic shifts in Vadodara. Minor expansion joints and grain color shifts are engineered into our joinery detailing to prevent buckling.
  • Hand-troweled lime plasters possess subtle micro-textures and sheen gradations created by individual artisan strokes.
04 / INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & ARCHITECTURAL DRAWINGS

Copyright and Single-Build License

All conceptual layouts, elevation drawings, 3D parametric simulations, structural working drawings, and bespoke joinery details created by DA STUDIOS remain the exclusive intellectual property and copyright of DA STUDIOS under the Copyright Act, 1957 of India.

Upon receipt of full contracted professional fees, the client is granted an exclusive, non-transferable, single-build license to execute the design specifically at the designated site location in Vadodara or designated municipality. Designs may not be duplicated, licensed to third parties, or re-used on other parcels without written authorization and royalty agreements.

05 / SITE ACCESS AND CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

Clear Title, Municipal Approvals, and Utilities

  • The client warrants unencumbered possession and legal ownership of the site plot or building unit.
  • The client is responsible for obtaining initial municipal building permissions (VMC / VUDA approvals), development permissions, and sanctioned floor space index (FSI) clearances, with technical drawing support provided by DA STUDIOS.
  • Continuous three-phase construction power, potable water connections, and dedicated lockable material storage areas must be made accessible to the atelier team at the site boundary.
06 / DEFECT LIABILITY & POST-HANDOVER GUARANTEE

Twelve-Month Atelier Guarantee

DA STUDIOS provides a twelve (12) month comprehensive Defect Liability Guarantee covering all bespoke joinery, concealed plumbing lines, and architectural fabrication executed directly by our team.

Branded fixtures (HVAC machinery, kitchen appliances, sanitaryware, luminaire drivers, architectural glass) carry the direct manufacturer warranties passed completely to the client.
