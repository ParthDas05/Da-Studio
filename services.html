DISCIPLINE 01

Full Turnkey Architecture

Comprehensive ground-up design and structural execution for private residential bungalows in Bhayli, Sevasi, and Gotri. We handle soil mechanics, VMC municipal sanctions, structural concrete, waterproofing guarantees, and complete envelope construction.

  • • Structural RCC and board-marked monolithic walls
  • • Solar-oriented cantilevers and passive cooling cross-drafts
  • • Acoustic double-glazed fenestration envelopes
  • • Locked construction schedules with milestone reporting
DISCIPLINE 02

Bespoke Interior Living

Tactile interior curation for luxury penthouses, duplex residences, and executive retreats. Continuous wire-brushed smoked oak joinery, concealed wardrobe systems, and hand-troweled slaked lime plaster crafted by our in-house Alkapuri atelier team.

  • • Concealed ceiling air-conditioning & linear slot diffusers
  • • Flos & Lutron museum-grade ambient lighting schemes
  • • Master joinery fabricated in controlled studio environment
  • • Ergonomic kitchen planning & Poliform-grade hardware
DISCIPLINE 03

1:1 BIM & Digital Simulation

We eliminate the discrepancy between 3D renderings and built handover through high-precision parametric BIM modeling. Every stone vein, curtain pocket, and MEP channel is verified dimensionally before physical site execution begins.

  • • Photorealistic daylight simulation calibrated to Vadodara solar paths
  • • Exact collision detection for plumbing and HVAC ducts
  • • Full virtual walkthrough before procurement release
  • • Direct CAD-to-millwork machine output
DISCIPLINE 04

Stone Provenance & Dry-Lay

Direct quarry block inspection and slab sequencing at Kishangarh and Italian warehouse hubs. Natural Roman Navona Travertine, Statuario marble, and charcoal quartzite are dry-laid on studio floors to verify vein harmony before site installation.

  • • Hand-inspected slab density and void filling checks
  • • Laser-guided bookmatching and continuous grain lines
  • • Anti-efflorescence sealing tailored to Gujarat groundwater
  • • Zero compromise on mineral thickness and calibration
