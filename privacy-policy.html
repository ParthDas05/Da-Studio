STATUTORY REFERENCE

Formulated in strict adherence to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023) of the Republic of India and professional Council of Architecture guidelines.

01 / COMMITMENT TO CONFIDENTIALITY

Discretion in Private Residential Architecture

DA STUDIOS ("we", "atelier", "studio") designs and constructs private residential properties, penthouses, and bespoke sanctuaries throughout Vadodara, Gujarat. We recognize that commissioning a bespoke residence requires sharing sensitive personal data, physical site coordinates, financial allocations, and lifestyle habits.

We adhere strictly to professional architectural non-disclosure practices and the provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 of India. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or publicly disseminate client information under any circumstance.

02 / INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Data Gathered During Architectural Engagement

A. Contact and Identity Data: Full legal name, telephone contact, email address, residential address, and preferred correspondence channels collected via our project specifier, atelier forms, or direct phone channels.

B. Property and Spatial Specifications: Plot boundaries, title documentation references, municipal zone surveys, site photographs, existing structure drawings, carpet areas, and orientation parameters for sites in Bhayli, Sevasi, Alkapuri, Gotri, or surrounding regions.

C. Financial and Feasibility Preferences: Anticipated budget parameters, material specification preferences (such as marble grading, millwork finishes, fenestration systems), and turnkey timeline objectives entered into our Feasibility Specifier or discussed during design consultations.

D. Technical Logs: Standard web server logs including IP address, browser type, and interaction metrics used strictly to maintain site performance and prevent fraudulent spam transmissions.

03 / HOW YOUR INFORMATION IS EMPLOYED

Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

  • To formulate tailored architectural proposals, 3D simulations, and project feasibility models.
  • To calculate detailed Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with accurate material rates for stone, timber, and joinery.
  • To coordinate municipal sanctions, structural engineering audits, and MEP clearances in Vadodara.
  • To maintain direct telephone, messaging, or written communications regarding site progress and atelier meetings.
  • To ensure secure verification of milestone payments and turnkey vendor procurement.
04 / ARCHITECTURAL PHOTOGRAPHY & ANONYMITY

Public Exhibition and Monograph Protocol

DA STUDIOS documents completed commissions for archival, monograph, and professional design documentation. To protect the privacy and physical security of our patrons:

  • Exact house numbers, street addresses, and identifiable family references are never disclosed publicly. Projects are cataloged under atelier nomenclature (for example: "The Courtyard Pavilion, Bhayli").
  • Private bedrooms, personal art inventories, and vault locations are strictly excluded from published plates unless explicitly approved in writing by the client.
  • Patrons retain the right to request full photographic embargoes prior to contract signing.
05 / SUB-CONTRACTORS & JOINERY FABRICATORS

Vetted Specialists and Non-Disclosure

Delivering high-precision turnkey residences requires sharing technical drawings with vetted structural engineers, MEP consultants, master carpenters, marble fabricators in Kishangarh, and fenestration suppliers. These parties receive only the dimensional and execution data strictly necessary to complete their specific work parcel. All partner specialists operate under confidentiality agreements preventing third-party re-sharing.
