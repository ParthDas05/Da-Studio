Philosophy & Method

Turning raw ideas into refined living. We reject mass templates to create bespoke, monolithic residences that respond deeply to Central Gujarat's light, geology, and spatial intimacy.

DESIGN CODE

Spatial Stillness

Luxury in private architecture is not about ornament; it is about stillness, proportion, and honesty of materials. When sunlight strikes hand-troweled lime plaster over raw travertine, the space speaks without loud decoration.

01 / SOLAR CALIBRATION & GUJARAT CLIMATE

Passive Cooling and Courtyard Aeration

Vadodara experiences intense, dry summer heat followed by heavy monsoon humidity. Rather than relying solely on mechanical air-conditioning, our residences use calculated orientation:

  • Heavy thermal buffers of board-marked concrete on south and west facades to absorb solar heat gain.
  • Deep verandahs and cantilevered roof overhangs that shade floor-to-ceiling glass during peak summer angles.
  • Central evaporative water courts that draw cool breezes across the ground living floor.
  • Operable clerestory vents that release rising hot air naturally throughout the afternoon.
02 / THE ALKAPURI JOINERY ATELIER

03 / MATERIAL ETHICS

Authenticity Over Imitation

We never use printed vinyl, faux-marble laminates, or artificial stone substitutes. Every material used in a DA STUDIOS residence carries geological honesty:

• Roman Navona Travertine: Dry-laid with natural voids filled with breathable mineral compound.
• Artisanal Slaked Lime: Five-pass troweled mineral plaster that matures and purifies ambient air.
• Smoked European Oak: Wire-brushed to reveal authentic tactile grain and deep organic hue.
• Cast Gunmetal Bronze: Custom door hardware that patinas gracefully with human touch over decades.
